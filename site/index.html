Locus Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

In short: what you write in Locus belongs to you. It stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We don't run servers that receive your data, we can't read your journal, and Locus has no ads, analytics, tracking, or accounts.

What Locus stores, and where

  • Journal entries are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Locus, they also sync through your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer of Locus cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Skills and exercises (worksheets, ratings, diary cards, plans, and similar records) are stored only on your device, in protected app storage. They are never synced or uploaded.
  • Unsaved drafts and app settings are stored only on your device.

AI features

Locus's optional AI features are off by default. When you turn them on, they use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your writing is processed on your device and is not sent to us or to any AI provider.

What Locus does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Things you choose to share

  • Export: you can create a PDF or JSON file of your records, for example to share with a therapist. The file only leaves the app when you share it yourself through the system share sheet, and it goes wherever you send it.
  • Links and phone numbers: crisis resources and the "find a therapist" link open in your phone, Messages, or browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Locus schedules reminders (for example, to check back on a plan) locally on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual entries or records inside the app.
  • Deleting Locus removes everything stored on the device.
  • To remove synced journal entries from iCloud, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage (or Storage), select Locus, and delete its data.

Beta testing

If you test Locus through TestFlight, Apple may share information with us such as your name, email, device model, crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the TestFlight terms. It never includes the contents of your journal.

Not medical care

Locus is a self-help journaling tool. It isn't therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it isn't a crisis service. If you might be in danger, call or text 988 (US), or your local emergency number.

Children

Locus is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Locus collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
